Key Trends Shaping 2026

The forces driving the next wave of AI transformation

01

Liquid Cooling Goes Mainstream

Rack densities hitting 240kW per rack in 2026 — rising to 1MW by 2028 — make liquid cooling unavoidable. Direct-to-chip and immersion tanks are now standard specs.

240kW/rack
02

Agentic AI Takes Over

Tools like n8n and Copilot Studio enable AI agents that actively perform work — sending emails, updating CRMs, and managing workflows with minimal human input.

Top trend
03

Edge AI Infrastructure

Placing AI workloads physically closer to users mitigates latency and boosts performance. Edge deployments are becoming critical for real-time applications.

Rising fast
04

Reasoning Over Retrieval

Models like OpenAI's o1 and o3 prioritize extended "thinking time" over simple retrieval, enabling deeper analysis and more reliable multi-step problem solving.

Paradigm shift
05

Digital Twins for Data Centers

Operators deploy digital twins to simulate complex systems, optimize cooling, predict failures, and achieve unprecedented operational efficiency.

Expanding
06

AI Robotics in Data Centers

Robotics now handle security monitoring, server installation, maintenance, cable organization, drive replacement, and liquid cooling optimization in modern facilities.

Emerging
